# Moodboard — Emily Carter, fine art & illustration portfolio

## Feel

Nostalgic, tactile, unapologetically maximalist. The one thing a visitor should\
feel first: "this is a real sketchbook/scrapbook that happens to be a website."

## Palette

- `--color-canvas: oklch(0.97 0.005 68)` — warm cream white (#f7f4f1), the\
  "paper" the collage sits on.
- `--color-ink: oklch(0.20 0.015 347)` — near-black plum (#1c1418), all text.
- `--color-accent: oklch(0.60 0.077 356)` — dusty mauve-pink (#a66d82), sampled\
  straight from the reference board; the dominant section-background color.
- `--color-accent-deep: oklch(0.30 0.07 359)` — deep burgundy (#4a1e2e), CTA\
  buttons, stamped badges, high-contrast text on the mauve sections.
- `--color-accent-soft: oklch(0.92 0.02 356)` — blush pink, card fills on cream\
  sections, hover states.\
  Ink-on-cream and cream-on-accent-deep both clear WCAG AA.

## Type

- Display: **Cabinet Grotesk** (800) — big condensed editorial headlines like\
  "ARTIST", stamped section titles.
- Body: **Satoshi** (400/500/700) — bio copy, captions, nav.
- Accent/handwriting flavor: a slight rotation + underline treatment on select\
  words instead of a script font (keeps load light, still feels handwritten).

## References

- Dorm-room scrapbook / Pinterest moodboard collages — washi-tape corners,\
  polaroid stacks, photobooth strips, hand-drawn stars and circles.
- 90s zine grunge texture: grain overlay on flat mauve blocks, torn-paper edges.
- Y2K digital-camera film aesthetic for the photography section.

## Structure (in order, named after the subject)

1. **Meet the Artist** — full-bleed portrait hero, overlaid stamped type, star\
   accent, scroll cue. (Departure: full-bleed image + overlaid type, not\
   text-left/image-right.)
2. **Hello, I'm Emily** — bio block + photobooth strip of portraits.
3. **Find Your Way** — a playful word-search-style nav grid where section\
   names are the hidden words, each one a real jump link.
4. **Style & Influences** — collage grid of mood/inspiration imagery under a\
   big stamped "STYLE" mark.
5. **Visuals — an eye for aesthetics** — photography strip, clipped to a\
   string like a darkroom line.
6. **The Gallery** — asymmetric bento grid of artwork with a pull-quote.\
   (Departure: one deep showcase grid + quote, not three testimonial cards.)
7. **Process & Sketchbook** — behind-the-scenes sketches / process shots /\
   a printed poster piece.
8. **Thank You** — closing band, portrait + big stamped "THANK YOU", contact\
   links. (Departure: CTA/contact band, not a form.)

## Departures (≥2, met)

- Hero: full-bleed portrait with overlaid stamped type + star, not the\
  text-left/image-right default.
- Proof slot: one deep gallery showcase + pull-quote instead of three\
  testimonial cards.
- Close slot: a thank-you CTA band with direct contact links, not a form.
